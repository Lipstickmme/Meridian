Santa Ana River Bridge Replacement

A three-span replacement crossing built over a flood control channel that has to stay clear through storm season.

Orange County, CA On site Client: County transportation authority 31 months

The existing crossing was scour-critical and two lanes short. The replacement is a three-span prestressed girder bridge on drilled shafts set back from the channel, so nothing new stands in the waterway. Falsework had to be out of the channel before each storm season, which set the pour sequence, and traffic stayed on the old structure until the new one carried load.
