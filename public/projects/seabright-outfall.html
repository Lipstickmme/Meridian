Marine project

Seabright Outfall Repair

A damaged subsea outfall located, clamped and welded in place, with the discharge never taken off line.

Monterey Bay, CA Completed 2023 Client: Regional water district 9 months

A dropped anchor had dented and cracked a section of outfall in 90 feet of water. We surveyed the full run by ROV, designed a split clamp that could be set and welded by divers against a live flow, and installed it in four dive days. Post-repair ultrasonic testing and a pressure survey confirmed the line back to full rated flow.
