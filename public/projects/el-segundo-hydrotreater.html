El Segundo Hydrotreater Revamp

A capacity revamp with 38 tie-ins into live systems, executed inside an eleven-day turnaround window.

El Segundo, CA Completed 2025 Client: Refinery operator 23 months

The revamp needed 38 cuts into systems that could only be opened during the annual turnaround. Every spool was fabricated, tested and laid down in erection order before the unit came off line, each tie-in was rehearsed against the scan-registered model, and the sequence was written so no two cuts competed for the same crane. The unit came back on specification on the first pass.
