Commercial project

Katella Medical Pavilion

An outpatient pavilion built hard against a working hospital, with the acute wing running the whole time.

Anaheim, CA Completed 2024 Client: Regional health system 26 months

The pavilion shares a wall, a plant room and an emergency vehicle route with a hospital that could not lose a single bed. Noise, vibration and infection control governed the programme more than the structure did: heavy work ran nights, the tie-in to the central plant was staged across four two-hour windows, and every penetration was sealed and pressure-tested before the shift ended.
