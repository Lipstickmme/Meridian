Commissioning and Handover

A project ends when the operator can run it without ringing us, not when the last valve is fitted.

Commissioning is planned from design stage, so the instrumentation needed to prove performance is in the drawings rather than added later.

Handover includes the as-built model, the test records and time with the people who will operate it. Most of the value of a digital model is realised after substantial completion.
