Mechanical and Process

Process plant lives or dies on layout. Pipe runs, access and maintenance decide the operating cost for the next thirty years.

We design piping systems and their supports, run stress analysis on hot lines, and lay out plant so that the units needing weekly attention are the ones a fitter can actually reach.

Energy work is measured, not assumed. We meter the existing plant first, then model the change, so the saving in the report is the saving on the bill.
