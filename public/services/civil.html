Major Civil Engineering

Ground is the least forgiving part of any project. We survey it, model it, and design what sits on it.

Our civil team takes a site from topographic survey through earthworks, drainage and pavement design to the point where a contractor can price it without a list of assumptions.

Flood and stormwater work is modelled against the rainfall the site will see in fifty years, not the rainfall it saw last year. That changes attenuation volumes, and it is cheaper to find out at design stage than at the first storm.
